## Today’s Buying Strategy

- **Stop buying $50–60 nine-booster “gift boxes.”** The evidence this week points the other way: *Reality Fracture* 30-pack boxes are available around $129, or roughly $4.30 per pack, while *Marvel Super Heroes* Jumpstart boxes are near a 90-day low. Buying small repackaged booster lots at $50–60 gives you worse pack economics and no meaningful sealed-product upside.[4][5]
- **Buy singles, not booster boxes, for finance.** Use a staged limit-order approach: identify a card’s recent supported range, place bids below the current ask, and buy in small tranches only after completed-sale evidence supports the price.
- **Best dip candidates from today’s evidence:** *Nethergoyf* on any pullback toward the high-single digits; *Gandalf, White Rider* only if it retraces toward the $6–7 area; *The Darkness Crystal* if it pulls back toward $5–6. These are demand-backed but still reprint-exposed.[1]
