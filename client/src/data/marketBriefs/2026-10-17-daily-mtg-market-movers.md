### Strategy for Today

- **Do not buy the $50–$60 nine-booster gift box as an investment vehicle.** You are paying for randomized inventory, many low-value cards, and sealed-product variance rather than selecting cards with verified demand.
- **Use a 70/20/10 budget split:** 70% for cards you will actually play or collect, 20% for proven format staples after a dip, and 10% for speculative penny cards.
- **Set limit orders or saved searches below current market:** Target approximately 10–15% below the lowest credible near-mint listing, but only when several sellers and recent sales support the reference price.
- **Buy in stages:** Purchase one-third at your first target, one-third if the card falls another 5–10%, and the final third only after a second event or deck-data confirmation.
- **Prefer broad utility:** Baleful Strix, Dispatch, and other cards with Commander, Pauper, Cube, or eternal applications are safer penny-pincher targets than narrow Standard cards.
- **Avoid pre-rotation accumulation:** Do not stockpile rotating Standard cards unless they have clear Commander or eternal demand.
- **Require a liquidity check:** Before buying, compare at least two marketplaces, inspect recent completed sales where available, and reject a card if the apparent gain comes from only one expensive listing.
- **Buy playsets, not boxes:** For a $50 budget, a focused list of four to eight useful singles is usually more controllable than nine random boosters.
- **Keep a streak tally:** Track exact printing, date, price, source, catalyst, and whether the move was confirmed, developing, speculative, or invalidated. A card should not count as a “streak” until it shows a verified move on at least two consecutive observations.
