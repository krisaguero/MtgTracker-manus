## Strategy for Today

### Buying plan

1. **Skip the $50–60 nine-booster gift boxes.** At roughly $5.56–$6.67 per booster before considering shipping, tax, and bulk-value losses, you are paying for lottery variance rather than selecting cards with known utility.
2. **Set a hard singles budget.** Use roughly 70% for proven Commander staples, 20% for format cards with repeated results, and 10% for speculative penny candidates.
3. **Buy in tranches.** Purchase one-third of the intended quantity today, another third after the weekend’s results, and the final third only if the card avoids a sharp supply-driven decline.
4. **Target dips, not green candles.** For new-set cards, wait for listings to normalize and buy when the lowest verified copies fall meaningfully below the previous week’s median—not merely when one seller posts a discount.
5. **Require liquidity.** Before purchasing, compare at least two marketplace listings, a market-price tracker, and a buylist or completed-sale signal where available.
6. **Prefer portable cards.** A card that fits five or more Commander archetypes is safer than a card that only upgrades Doom Prevails or one new commander.
7. **Use the “playset or pass” rule for Standard.** Buy only cards you need for a deck or cards showing repeated four-of adoption across several successful lists.
8. **Avoid sealed-product averaging.** If you want sealed exposure, wait for discounts, bundles with meaningful promos, or post-release clearance rather than buying regular booster gift boxes at retail.

### Practical buy thresholds

- **Commander staple:** Buy gradually when the card is at or below the lower end of its post-release range and has broad cross-deck usage.
- **Standard card:** Buy only after repeated event adoption and before a major paper tournament, not after one online result.
- **Premium treatment:** Buy only when the premium is supported by collector demand and not merely low inventory.
- **Penny card:** Cap purchases at a small fixed amount and assume most will remain bulk.
- **Reprint-exposed card:** Prefer copies for personal use; do not treat a cheap price alone as an investment thesis.
