# MTG Market Watch — Top Performing Cards

**As of:** October 22, 2026  
**Scope:** Available October 1–22 daily snapshots and October 13–22 editorial Market Watch logs  
**Market status:** Snapshot-derived hobby-market analysis; not financial advice

## Executive summary

The strongest recent performance is concentrated in inexpensive Commander-oriented cards and basic utility cards. The largest single-session move was **Temple of Triumph, TDC #407, at +157.89% to $0.49** on October 22. The strongest repeat winner across consecutive full-width snapshots was **Swamp, ZNR #382**, which moved **+127.27% to $0.75** on October 21 and another **+58.67% to $1.19** on October 22.

The most convincing short-window repeat performers were **Panther Robot, Bast, Panther Goddess, Mirkwood Bats, Annie Joins Up, Sulfur Falls, and Swamp**. These cards rose on both October 21 and October 22, unlike several apparent leaders that reversed sharply after a one-day spike.

The data also shows a major distinction between magnitude and durability. The October 21–22 leaders are mostly sub-$2 cards, so percentage changes are highly sensitive to small absolute price changes and thin inventory. **Tivit, Seller of Secrets, NCC #10**, remains the largest editorial spike in the available research posts at **+233.33% to $15**, but it was observed only once and should be treated as a high-impact, low-repeatability signal rather than a confirmed trend.

## Earlier October persistence

The smaller nine-signal daily logs from October 1–16 show a different type of performer than the full snapshots from October 20–22:

- **Counterspell — MH2 #267** appeared repeatedly and was the most persistent early-month mover. It moved through positive and negative sessions, including **−19.53% on Oct. 10**, **−13.27% on Oct. 11**, then **+9.70% on Oct. 12** and **+11.90% on Oct. 13**. This is a volatile reprint/supply signal, not a clean upward trend.
- **The Great Henge — ELD #161** appeared throughout the early logs but stayed close to flat, generally within small positive or negative changes. It is better classified as a monitored high-value baseline than a top performer.
- **Tyvar, the Pummeler — PDSK #202p** produced repeated positive observations, including +3.41%, +5.48%, and smaller follow-through moves. The magnitude was modest but more repeatable than many penny spikes.
- **Preeminent Captain — M15 #25** appeared in two editorial reports at +2.74%. Its repeat appearance is useful for tracking, but the move itself was small.
- **Command Tower — CMD #269** produced a sharp reversal pattern: −15.69% in the daily logs, while the broader Command Tower family also appeared in later positive movement. The set and collector number must not be merged across printings.

## Best candidates by use case

### Strongest repeat-momentum watch

**Swamp — ZNR #382** is the clearest current repeat-momentum signal. It rose on two consecutive full snapshots and reached $1.19. The correct next step is not to chase the move; verify whether sales volume and multiple marketplaces support the increase and whether this is a specific treatment or inventory event.

### Best cluster to monitor

The **MSC low-cost / Commander-adjacent cluster** deserves monitoring: Panther Robot, Bast, Panther Goddess, Annie Joins Up, Beast Within, Arcane Signet, and other MSC printings appeared repeatedly among the October 21–22 gainers. Their common behavior suggests a release, inventory, or pricing-normalization effect may be influencing the cohort. The dataset does not identify a shared catalyst, so this is a cluster observation rather than a causal conclusion.

### Strongest higher-dollar observation

**The Theorist, Jace Beleren — FRA #43** at $63.41 and **Tivit, Seller of Secrets — NCC #10** at $15 are more meaningful in absolute-dollar terms than sub-$1 penny risers. Both remain single-observation editorial spikes in the available records, so they should be treated as developing rather than confirmed.

### Best “do not chase” group

The October 22 triple-digit penny risers—Temple of Triumph, Sulfur Falls, Hammer of Purphoros, Reality Shift, Goblin Electromancer, Octomancer, Loyal Apprentice, and Guttersnipe—should remain **monitor/watch**, not automatic buys. Their percentages are impressive, but their latest prices are mostly $0.49–$0.62 and may be dominated by thin listings or small absolute price changes.

## Data limitations

1. October 20–22 snapshots contain 12,511 signals each, while October 1–16 snapshots contain only nine tracked records per day. Rankings across those periods are therefore not directly comparable in breadth.
2. No daily snapshot files were available for October 17–19 in the local archive.
3. Percentage changes are snapshot-derived and do not establish completed-sale volume, liquidity, condition, finish, language, or treatment.
4. Several cards are below $1, where small absolute changes create very large percentages.
5. The Oct. 22 Constructed Format Watch used the new transparent fallback because its topic filter returned zero cards; those cards are broad top movers, not verified constructed-format performers.
6. Exact printings are separated by set code and collector number in this report. Regular, foil, borderless, showcase, and other treatments require additional treatment-level verification before transacting.

## Recommended follow-up

- Keep Swamp ZNR #382, Panther Robot MSC #113, Bast MSC #75, Mirkwood Bats LTR #95, Annie Joins Up MSC #281, and Sulfur Falls SOC #408 on a 3–7 day confirmation list.
- Require at least one additional positive snapshot before upgrading a one-day penny spike from **Watch** to **Developing**.
- Review the MSC cohort as a group for shared supply or decklist effects, without assuming a common catalyst.
- Treat Counterspell MH2 #267 and Command Tower variants as volatility/reprint studies rather than straightforward gainers.
- Use exact printing, current price, source count, and completed-sale evidence before making a purchase decision.
