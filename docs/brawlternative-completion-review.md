# Brawlternative completion review

## Scope and format

These are **custom 60-card Commander-preview Brawlternatives**, not official Magic Brawl and not regulation 100-card Commander decks. The submitted source records remain intact under `decks`; the separate `proposedDecks` records are editorial revisions and do not assert ownership, proxy coverage, or playtesting results.

## Proposals

### The Wise Mothman — 60 cards

**Exact choices:** correct `Harold and Bob, First City` → `Harold and Bob, First Numens`, `Strong, the Brutish Companion` → `Strong, the Brutish Thespian`, and `Piper Wright, Public Reporter` → `Piper Wright, Publick Reporter` using current Scryfall names. Remove off-identity `Abzan Falconer` (white identity). Add `Fathom Mage` for counter-based card flow and `Inspiring Call` for budget protection plus draw. The result is 60 cards and keeps the 22-land source base.

**Rationale:** the proposal fixes names without rewriting the submission, enforces Sultai identity, and fills the missing 59th-card problem with two synergistic, lower-cost engine pieces rather than premium staples. Fathom Mage draws when counters are placed on it; Inspiring Call rewards the distributed +1/+1-counter board and protects it.

### Rakdos, Lord of Riots — 60 cards / 21 lands

**Exact choices:** retain all submitted cards, including `Leechridden Swamp`; the proposal is a complete 60-card list with 21 lands. Correct the timing explanation: an opponent must lose life earlier in the same turn before Rakdos can be cast; after Rakdos resolves, his reduction applies only to later creature spells that turn and reduces generic mana only.

**Rationale:** no speculative swap is needed to complete the source. `Leechridden Swamp` enters tapped, requires two black permanents, and costs `{B}` plus tapping to make each opponent lose 1 life. It is therefore a repeatable setup line, not a free or retroactive discount and not a way to pay Rakdos’s initial colored pips in the same activation.

### Karlov of the Ghost Council — 60 cards

**Exact choices:** apply the user-approved `Crested Sunmare` correction and `Daxos the Returned` replacement for Licia; retain `Daxos, Blessed by the Sun` as a separate life-gain engine card. No other slot changes.

**Rationale:** the proposed record makes the approved corrections explicit while preserving the source’s 20-land, Orzhov shell. Karlov’s exile ability still costs `{W}{B}` plus six counters; life-gain triggers do not grant evasion. The combo and expensive finishers remain disclosed as hypotheses requiring playtesting.
