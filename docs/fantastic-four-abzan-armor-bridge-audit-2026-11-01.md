# Fantastic Four ↔ Abzan Armor bridge audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-01  
**Target deck:** The Fantastic Four (`msc`) — Invisible Woman  
**Source deck:** Abzan Armor (`tdc`) — Felothar the Steadfast

## Executive finding

The archived decklists share **10 exact card names**, but those cards are mostly generic mana and land infrastructure. They are not automatically transferable: moving one from Abzan Armor would make the source deck incomplete unless a duplicate or replacement is available.

The useful ecosystem bridge is a separate **Abzan Wall/toughness package**. None of the 12 audited bridge cards are currently in the stock Fantastic Four list, so they represent genuine upgrade candidates rather than duplicates already present in the target deck.

The current authenticated inventory evidence available in this workspace previously showed only two copies of Giada, Font of Hope and no verified Fantastic Four overlap. A full ManaBox export is still required before any card can be marked as physically transferable.

## Recommended Abzan Armor → Invisible Woman bridge

These cards are in Abzan Armor and absent from the stock Fantastic Four list. They create the **Wall conversion** lane described in the upgrade guide.

### Core engine

1. **Assault Formation** — primary toughness-to-power engine; also provides a pump mode.
2. **Tower Defense** — one-turn toughness boost that can convert a defensive board into lethal combat.
3. **Axebane Guardian** — turns a critical mass of defenders into colored mana.
4. **Overgrown Battlement** — additional defender-based mana acceleration.

### High-value Wall bodies

5. **Wall of Reverence** — defensive body with a life-gain payoff that buys time for Invisible Woman’s engine.
6. **Wall of Omens** — replaces itself while increasing defender density.
7. **Wall of Blossoms** — another cantrip Wall that improves the conversion package without creating card disadvantage.
8. **Wall of Roots** — early defense plus flexible mana production.
9. **Weathered Sentinels** — scaling defender that can become a credible attacker under a toughness engine.
10. **Crashing Drawbridge** — gives a wide Wall board a haste-like finishing turn.
11. **Towering Titan** — a high-toughness threat that benefits from the conversion plan.
12. **Wall of Limbs** — a thematic Wall body with a late-game mana/life payoff.

## Suggested transfer sequence

Do not move all 12 cards at once. Use staged packages:

### Package A — prove the plan

- Assault Formation
- Wall of Omens
- Wall of Blossoms
- Axebane Guardian
- Tower Defense

This is the minimum test package. It adds a toughness engine, two card-replacing Walls, mana support, and a combat-finisher turn.

### Package B — stabilize the shell

Add:

- Wall of Roots
- Overgrown Battlement
- Wall of Reverence
- Weathered Sentinels

This package increases defender density and gives the deck more time to assemble Invisible Woman plus its protection and token engines.

### Package C — finish the conversion

Add:

- Crashing Drawbridge
- Towering Titan
- Wall of Limbs

These are the more optional slots. Keep them only if the playgroup and final list want a dedicated Wall identity rather than a lighter defensive subtheme.

## Transfer rules

- A sealed precon being marked owned does **not** make its cards transferable.
- A card in both decklists is not a free copy; it is a shared requirement.
- A scanned card is transferable only when the scanned quantity exceeds the copies reserved for active source decks.
- Basic lands should be handled separately from exact-printing staples.
- A transfer should be recorded as `borrow` or `substitute` in the upgrade ledger, not silently removed from Abzan Armor.
- The full ManaBox export is required to turn this audit from a theoretical bridge into an exact inventory-backed package.

## Bottom line

The honest current result is:

- **10 exact shared staples/lands**
- **12 Abzan Wall/toughness upgrade candidates**
- **0 verified transferable copies from the current authenticated evidence**
- **5-card minimum test package recommended first**

Invisible Woman should borrow the Wall engine—not strip generic mana and lands from Abzan Armor.
