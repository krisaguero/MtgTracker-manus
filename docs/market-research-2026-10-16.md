# MTG Market Research — 2026-10-16

## Evidence captured

- TCGplayer’s October 15 weekly price-spike article identifies Reality Fracture-driven Standard demand around The Theorist, Jace Beleren, plus movement in Abstergo Entertainment, Haliya, Ripples of Potential, and Tivit, Seller of Secrets. It reports Jace at $63.41, up from about $35; Abstergo above $4 after roughly $2; Ripples from roughly $3 to $6; Tivit near $15 from a long-held $4–$5 range; and Haliya near $6 without a clean prior baseline.
- TCGIndex’s October 16 market report puts the broad MTG market at $779,602, up 0.4% over seven days and 2.8% over 30 days, with 52% of tracked cards advancing. It identifies The Theorist, Jace Beleren as the biggest mover at +35.4% and around $82.26. The conflict with TCGplayer’s $63.41 is recorded as a data-quality issue, not averaged away.
- Wizards’ Foundations Commander announcement confirms five ready-to-play monocolor decks released alongside Reality Fracture on October 2, 2026, with preorder availability through local game stores, TCGplayer, Amazon, and other retailers.
- Wizards’ Mystery Booster Commander Edition announcement confirms availability at MagicCon Atlanta on November 13–15 and at future Magic events. It is a Commander Draft booster product, not a ready-to-play precon.
- Wizards’ Festival in a Box: Atlanta announcement sets the sale date to October 19, 2026, while supplies last, with Mystery Booster Commander Edition and Secret Lair content.

## Data-quality decisions

- Jace’s current price is stored as a range/disagreement in Notion rather than a single normalized truth.
- Haliya has no calculated percentage because the source did not provide a clean prior baseline.
- Exact collector numbers for Reality Fracture cards are unresolved in this pass.
- Tivit is marked do-not-chase because the reported move is large and deck-specific.
- Precon and sealed-product announcements are tracked separately from portable single-card demand.
