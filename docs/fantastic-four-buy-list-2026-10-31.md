# Fantastic Four — Invisible Woman Buy-List

**Research snapshot:** October 31, 2026
**Target shell:** *The Fantastic Four* — Invisible Woman, Sue Storm
**Objective:** Upgrade the stock four-color Marvel Super Heroes Commander deck while prioritizing cards that reinforce noncreature-spell casting, Wall/token production, triggered-ability copying, and protection.

## Executive recommendation

Do not start by buying every high-priced EDHREC recommendation. The precon already contains the deck’s foundational ramp and several strong Marvel payoffs, including **Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Farseek, Cultivate, Three Visits, Expressive Iteration, Invisible Force Field, Valeria Richards, Franklin Richards, Mister Fantastic, The Fantasticar, First Family,** and the major stock sweepers.

The first purchase should be a compact **12-card functional package**: efficient protection and interaction plus repeatable noncreature-spell payoffs. That package is much more likely to improve the deck immediately than buying premium token doublers before the shell has enough reliable spell velocity.

The best first commander choice for this plan remains **Mister Fantastic, Reed Richards** if you want maximum flexibility and trigger-copying. Keep **Invisible Woman** as the commander when you want a more defensive go-wide Wall/token plan. **The Thing** and **Human Torch** are alternate rebuild paths, not cards that need to be bought for the baseline upgrade.

### First-wave cut direction

Use the first wave to remove the cards that are least connected to the chosen commander’s plan rather than cutting all expensive cards. The cleanest early cuts are usually the slower, off-plan payoffs and redundant combat-only cards: **Galactus, Devourer of Worlds; Silver Surfer, Galactus’s Herald;** and other cards that do not reward noncreature spells, Wall/token production, or triggered-ability copying. Card-for-card cuts should be finalized after a few games because the deck’s preferred commander changes the correct curve.

## Commander-specific paths

### Invisible Woman — defensive go-wide

Buy **Heroic Intervention, Propaganda, Shark Typhoon, Parallel Lives, Caretaker’s Talent, Impact Tremors, High Alert,** and **Cryptolith Rite**. The deck wants to cast a noncreature spell before combat, create or multiply Wall/token bodies, and turn a defensive board into a lethal swing or a table-wide damage engine.

### Mister Fantastic — value and trigger-copying

Buy **Counterspell, An Offer You Can’t Refuse, Storm-Kiln Artist, Archmage Emeritus, Shark Typhoon, Lithoform Engine, Improvisation Capstone,** and **Echocasting Symposium**. This version is the most flexible and is the best default upgrade path if you want one deck that can change direction without a full rebuild.

### The Thing — counters and combat

Buy **Doubling Season** only if you want this to become a dedicated counters deck. Add **Caretaker’s Talent** and **Cryptolith Rite** as supporting engines, then prioritize protection so the commander survives long enough to multiply counters. This path is more expensive and less directly supported by the stock list than the Mister Fantastic path.

### Human Torch — Voltron and combat damage

Buy **Swiftfoot Boots** first, then prioritize protection, evasion, and noncreature spells that create a safe attack. This is the most commander-dependent path; do not buy the token-doubling package for it unless you are intentionally changing the deck’s identity.

## Cards not to buy yet

Do not buy another copy of **Arcane Signet, Sol Ring, Farseek, Cultivate, Three Visits, Expressive Iteration, First Family, Invisible Force Field, Valeria Richards, Franklin Richards, Mister Fantastic, The Fantasticar, Negative Zone Portal, Path of Discovery, Bovine Intervention, Collective Effort,** or **Galactus, Devourer of Worlds** for this deck. They are already present in the reconciled precon list.

Also defer generic premium staples such as **Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Rhystic Study,** and **Smothering Tithe** unless the purchase is intended to serve multiple decks in the broader collection.
