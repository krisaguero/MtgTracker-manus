## 2026-08-22 Commander pricing audit

- MTGJSON’s official Deck model defines `commander`, `mainBoard`, `sideBoard`, `name`, `code`, `releaseDate`, and `sealedProductUuids` fields. The deck model documentation is at https://mtgjson.com/data-models/deck/.
- MTGJSON’s All Decks page is at https://mtgjson.com/downloads/all-decks/ and exposes individual JSON downloads including current Commander product filenames such as `AvengersAssemble_MSC.json`, `DoomPrevails_MSC.json`, `TheFantasticFour_MSC.json`, and `WakandaForever_MSC.json`.
- The project’s local `commanderDecklistsData.ts` then contained skeletal entries (commander plus a few cards), so its totals could not represent full products. This is a historical audit note, not the current deck-catalog status.
- After filtering the official MTGJSON All Decks catalog to `Commander Deck`, the page reported 190 results. The filtered results included the standard products and separate Collector’s Edition variants; those are distinct products only when the product catalog intentionally exposes them.
- With the Commander Deck filter applied and Show All expanded, MTGJSON reported 190 entries. The live catalog confirmed that several local names were stale: Final Fantasy products were `Counter Blitz`, `Limit Break`, `Revival Trance`, and `Scions & Spellcraft`; Edge of Eternities products were `Counter Intelligence` and `World Shaper`; Tarkir Dragonstorm used five clans; and collector editions were separately listed.
- Official MTGJSON deck payload probe: `https://mtgjson.com/api/v5/decks/AvengersAssemble_MSC.json` contains 1 commander, 99 mainboard cards by quantity, `sealedProductUuids`, per-card `identifiers.scryfallId`, `setCode`, `number`, and retailer purchase URLs, but no direct `prices` object on the deck card records.
- Official MTGJSON set payload probe: `https://mtgjson.com/api/v5/MSC.json` includes `sealedProduct` records with `cardCount`, product name, release date, `uuid`, `tcgplayerProductId`, and purchase URLs, but no direct sealed MSRP field. Sealed-price comparisons therefore need an explicit nullable sealed-price field and provenance rather than a fabricated default.
- The project price index then had 79 daily signals and 175 Scryfall refresh products; that was not complete coverage for a 100-card Commander deck. Full-deck pricing must show priced/unpriced counts and must not treat missing rows as zero.
- The official bulk price artifact is `https://mtgjson.com/api/v5/AllPricesToday.json.gz`. A streamed probe for a known Commander card UUID returned paper retail normal prices under `tcgplayer`, `cardkingdom`, and `cardmarket`, with date key `2026-08-21` and USD/EUR currency metadata. Preserve vendor currency: Cardmarket values remain EUR and are not used as a USD quote.

## 2026-10-16 release data boundaries

- The public Commander index is sourced from MTGJSON `AllPricesToday`; quote date is **2026-10-16**. Coverage metadata reports **13,323 printings total**, **13,061 with a resolved USD quote**, and **262 unresolved**. USD valuation prefers TCGplayer USD and falls back to Card Kingdom USD. Cardmarket values remain EUR and are excluded from USD coverage.
- The imported Daily Movers table is a distinct **250-row user-provided CSV snapshot dated 2026-08-25**. It is historical and is not refreshed by the page.
- `data/market/daily/2026-10-15.json` is a separate **nine-printing MTGJSON sample dated 2026-10-15**. Its initial observation is a baseline, not a movement.
- `client/src/data/dailyMarketSnapshot.json` contains **14 source-dated signal records**. Their observation window is **2026-10-08 through 2026-10-16**; file generation time is not substituted for a row’s observation date.
- `client/src/data/notionMarketWatchSnapshot.json` is a static read-only research capture, captured `2026-10-17T02:14:17Z` with source evidence through `2026-10-16`. It includes only public research, signal fields, official announcements, review outcomes, and public citations. It excludes workspace IDs/relations, collections, scans, order/proxy queues, and BYOK keys; it does not auto-refresh.
- The legacy morning report and its generated sample sentiment/percentage moves are paused. Empty source data produces no canonical mover rows; the app does not create seeded prices or current timestamps. Existing browser-local archive entries are not deleted or rewritten.
- No Supabase settings or schema were changed. Collection, order/proxy queues, BYOK keys, and scans remain in their existing browser-local paths and are not included in the public research snapshot.
