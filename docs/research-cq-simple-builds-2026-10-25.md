Source video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x8KK0-U8FAc

Source page metadata fetched October 25, 2026: title “Commanders With Super Simple Builds That Will DOMINATE Your Playgroup!”, channel The Commander’s Quarters, 34,921 views, published May 31, 2026. Public chapter list showed: Introduction 0:00; Jasmine Boreal of the Seven 0:36; Yargle and Multani 2:59; Arcades the Strategist 5:11; Edric, Spymaster of Trest 7:10; Ovika, Enigma Goliath 8:58; Omnath, Locus of Mana 10:57; Mr. Orfeo the Boulder 12:46. The transcript continues with Ruric Thar, the Unbowed around 15:06 even though the fetched chapter display did not include that later chapter.

A separate MTG Daily Drop page used a similar title but a different ten-commander list and separate chapter timings: https://mtgdailydrop.com/commanders-with-super-simple-builds-that-dominate-your-playgroup/. It names Fynn, Alela, Goreclaw, Torbran, Jarad, Memnarch, Jetmir, The Locust God, Dina, and Anzrag. Do not merge that list into the x8KK0-U8FAc article without separately verifying that video.

Transcript excerpts and timestamps from the x8KK0-U8FAc video:
- 0:17–0:25: “commanders that have super simple builds that will absolutely dominate your play group.”
- 0:36–1:01: Jasmine is described as an underrated commander; vanilla creatures are difficult to block under her condition. “Your creatures are near unblockable.”
- 1:30–1:44: Unnatural Growth is described as doubling power and toughness at the beginning of each combat.
- 2:41–2:58: Yargle and Multani is an 18/6 with the simple plan of reaching 21 commander damage; Indomitable Might and Rogue’s Passage help push damage through.
- 4:55–5:05: “a super simple, super straightforward commander that says, ‘Hey, can you count to 21?’”
- 5:11–6:59: Arcades turns defenders’ toughness into combat damage and draws cards when defenders enter; the suggested shell is cheap high-toughness walls plus toughness pumps and blink/protection.
- 7:10–8:54: Edric rewards evasive low-to-the-ground creatures; combat damage draws cards. “Get a bunch of evasive low-to-the-ground creatures out, swing, draw an insane amount of cards, utilize some pump effects.”
- 8:58–10:50: Ovika makes hasty Phyrexian Goblins from noncreature spells; the simple shell focuses on mana rocks, card draw, and pump effects.
- 10:57–12:42: Omnath stores unspent green mana, grows from it, and supports a Voltron-style plan; Wilderness Reclamation and Mana Reflection amplify the mana bank.
- 12:46–15:03: Mr. Orfeo doubles a target creature’s power when attacking; the “Hotline Fling” plan uses large creatures, power doublers, and sacrifice-to-damage effects.
- 15:06 onward: Ruric Thar punishes noncreature spells for six damage; the shell is creature-heavy and uses creature-only ramp such as Somberwald Sage. Nikya of the Old Ways is mentioned as a supporting example in the Ruric Thar section, not as a separate chapter in this video’s fetched chapter display.
