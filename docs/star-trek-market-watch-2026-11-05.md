## What changed

The Market Movers page now includes a source-dated **Star Trek release watch** overlay. The overlay includes the noted Reality Fracture, Commander, Standard, premium-foil, and Star Trek singles. Each row has a portrait card frame, an internal Market Tracker link, a source link, a price when the brief supplied one, the reported move, evidence status, thesis, risk, and verified window.

## Star Trek product context

Wizards’ official decklist and collecting pages confirm a November 13 tabletop release with four Commander decks: **Federation Fleet**, **Landing Party**, **Klingon Fury**, and **We Are the Borg**. The panel shows the official MSRP references recorded in the supplied brief context: $74.99 for a Commander deck and $159.99 for a Collector’s Edition deck. These are product-price references, not claims about current secondary-market prices.

## Signal interpretation

The clearest Star Trek pattern is not a buy signal. Quark, Khan, Dr. Beverly Crusher, Starfleet Academy, Hallowed Fountain, and related rows show severe pre-release declines or volatility. The appropriate posture is to wait for November 13 supply, then recheck completed sales, listing depth, Commander adoption, and EDHREC inclusion 3–7 days after release.

The confirmed positive non-Star Trek rows—Preston, Aminatou’s rainbow-foil treatment, and Nicol Bolas, Dragon-God—are retained as context for the broader Reality Fracture/Jace demand thesis. Premium-foil and thin-listing rows are labeled speculative or supply-risk even when the reported percentage move is large.
